# Varian cerita + seleksi pairwise internal

Bounty: Two-Sentence Horror (u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253).
Kriteria juri (leksikografis, RULES.txt): (1) tepat dua kalimat + benar-benar menakutkan;
(2) kalimat kedua memutar/mengungkap sesuatu yang membuat kalimat pertama lebih buruk saat
dibaca ulang; (3) horor tersirat, bukan dijelaskan; (4) prosa presisi & konkret;
(5) orisinal, bukan twist familiar. Margin juri 1–5: pilih varian yang menang TELAK.

Aturan build (rank-recovery §10g): struktur 2-beat + reveal di klausa terakhir WAJIB.
Tiap varian diuji: "di mana twist-nya?" — tanpa twist, buang. Pakai angka spesifik dan/atau
karakter bernama.

## Varian yang ditulis (8)

### V1 — Library (buku perpustakaan)
> I returned the library book my mother borrowed the week she died, thirty years overdue, and the librarian stamped it without looking up. She smiled and said, 'Your mother renewed this one yesterday — she always comes in on Thursdays.'
- Twist: ibu yang mati 30 tahun lalu masih memperpanjang buku, tiap Kamis. Angka: 30 tahun, Kamis.
- Kelemahan: dread yang tenang; kurang "menakutkan" secara viseral.

### V2 — Swimming lessons (JUARA)
> The new lifeguard stopped me after Lily's lesson to say she is the best swimmer he has ever taught. She drowned in that pool when she was four, and she has had a lesson every Tuesday since her funeral.
- Twist: anak yang tenggelam umur 4 tahun masih les renang tiap Selasa sejak pemakamannya.
  Kalimat 1 (pujian lifeguard) dibaca ulang menjadi horor: yang dia ajar bukan anak hidup.
- Angka/nama: Lily (bernama), umur 4, tiap Selasa, sejak pemakaman. "New" di lifeguard
  menjelaskan kenapa dia tidak tahu. Ironi: perenang terbaik = anak yang tenggelam.
- Horor tersirat: tidak dijelaskan apa Lily sekarang / siapa yang mengajar — pembaca mengisi.

### V3 — Barber (tukang cukur)
> The barber has cut my hair every month for ten years, and today he asked, as he always does, how my father is. I told him, as I always do, that my father died before I was born — and he said, 'Then who has been sitting in the waiting chair, watching you, every single time?'
- Twist: seseorang duduk mengawasiku tiap kali cukur selama 10 tahun. Angka: tiap bulan, 10 tahun.
- Kelemahan: "watching you" agak samar; frasa "died before I was born" menggemakan entry
  field ("dated 1987 — nineteen years before I was born").

### V4 — Confessional (pengakuan dosa)
> I confessed to the priest that I have started sleepwalking, and twice now I have woken up standing over my wife's side of the bed. He told me to pray and come back next week, and this morning the parish office called to ask why I confessed to an empty booth — the church has had no priest for nine years.
- Twist ganda: aku sleepwalking di atas ranjang istri (aku sendiri ancamannya) + tidak ada
  pendeta selama 9 tahun (jadi siapa yang menyuruhku berdoa?). Angle segar (tidak ada entry
  gereja/pendeta di field).
- Kelemahan: dua twist saling mengencerkan; kalimat 2 panjang dan berbelit.

### V5 — Airbnb (tuan rumah)
> Every guest in my spare room leaves a five-star review praising the host — quiet, kind, always there when you need him. I live alone and have never met a single guest, so I asked the last one who let her in, and she said: you did.
- Twist: "you did" — seseorang yang identik denganku menunjukkan tamu berkeliling rumahku.
- Kelemahan: mekanisme butuh inferensi lebih; horornya tidak langsung.

### V6 — Ultrasound (USG)
> The ultrasound showed our baby waving at the screen, and the technician laughed and said she waves just like her father. My husband died eleven months ago — three months before she was conceived.
- Twist: paradoks waktu (hamil dari suami yang mati 3 bulan sebelum pembuahan).
- Kelemahan: lebih "teka-teki uncanny" daripada menakutkan; trope ayah-mati umum; pembaca
  harus berhitung (11 − 3 = 8 bulan hamil).

### V20 — First word (kata pertama)
> My daughter said her first word today — 'Elias' — and my wife went pale, because Elias was the name we chose for the son we lost before she was born. We have never said that name aloud, not once, in this house.
- Twist: anak tahu nama yang tak pernah diucapkan di rumah — dari mana? Nama spesifik (Elias).
- Kelemahan: dread tenang; trope "anak tahu hal yang tak seharusnya" cukup dikenal.

### V8 — Drew me twice (gambar anak)
> My son's teacher called to say he drew our family again today, and this time he drew me twice. The second me was standing behind the first, with its hands on my shoulders.
- Twist: sesuatu berdiri di belakangku, tangan di bahuku. Gambar spesifik.
- Kelemahan: klaster "anak menggambar keluarga + figur ekstra" sudah ramai di field
  ("My Son Drew Our Family", "The Extra Child in Every Photo").

### V15 — Carbon monoxide alarm
> The carbon monoxide alarm woke us at 2 a.m., and I carried the baby out into the snow while my wife called the fire crew. They found no gas — and no battery in the alarm, either; I removed it three years ago, the night my father died in this house.
- Twist: alarm tanpa baterai tetap berbunyi — sesuatu memperingatkan kami.
- Kelemahan: entitasnya protektif (memperingatkan), bukan mengancam → kurang menakutkan;
  klaster alarm asap sudah ada di field; reveal (tanpa baterai) tidak di klausa terakhir.

## Seleksi pairwise (juara: V2)

Kriteria: leksikografis RULES.txt + margin (pilih yang menang TELAK).

| Pasangan | Pemenang | Margin | Alasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| V2 vs V1 | V2 | ~3 | V2 viseral (anak mati di kolam, les berlanjut); V1 dread tenang. Kriteria 1: V2 lebih menakutkan. |
| V2 vs V3 | V2 | ~3–4 | V3 samar ("watching you"); V2 konkret dan horornya langsung. |
| V2 vs V4 | V2 | ~2 | V4 dua twist saling mengencerkan + kalimat 2 berbelit; V2 satu twist, mendarat penuh. Kriteria 4: V2 lebih ketat. |
| V2 vs V5 | V2 | ~3 | V5 butuh inferensi; horor V2 langsung. |
| V2 vs V20 | V2 | ~2–3 | V20 (kata pertama "Elias", tak pernah diucapkan) tenang dan ketat, tapi V2 lebih menakutkan. |
| V2 vs V15 | V2 | ~4 | V15 protektif (entitas memperingatkan) → kurang menakutkan; reveal tidak di klausa terakhir. |
| V2 vs V6 | V2 | ~3 | V6 teka-teki paradoks, bukan horor; trope ayah-mati. |
| V2 vs V8 | V2 | ~3 | V8 bagus tapi klaster gambar-keluarga ramai; V2 angle segar (tidak ada entry les renang/tenggelam di field). |

V2 menang di semua pasangan dengan margin 2–4 — menang TELAK, bukan tipis.

## Cerita final (dipakai di main.py)

> The new lifeguard stopped me after Lily's lesson to say she is the best swimmer he has ever taught. She drowned in that pool when she was four, and she has had a lesson every Tuesday since her funeral.

- Tepat 2 kalimat, 201 karakter, 201 byte (verifikasi programatik di bawah).
- Cek kriteria: (1) dua kalimat, menakutkan — anak mati masih les renang; (2) kalimat 2
  memutar pujian lifeguard menjadi horor; (3) tersirat — tidak dijelaskan apa Lily sekarang;
  (4) konkret — Lily, umur 4, tiap Selasa, pemakaman; (5) orisinal — tidak ada entry les
  renang/tenggelam di 475 submission; bukan twist familiar (bukan cermin/baby-monitor/
  smart-speaker/ketukan 3 pagi).
- Bukan gore, restraint > shock, sesuai brief juri.
